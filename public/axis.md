# Axis

**Axis** is a new Discord bot in development and the planned successor to **Vortex**.
It is being created so that **Vortex**'s work can continue beyond the end of the
current bot.

## Why Axis is being made

Vortex is expected to stop operating on **December 27, 2026**. This is because
I can no longer access its Discord developer team, which prevents
me from doing the verification and intent requests needed to keep **Vortex**
running.

The original plan was to let **Vortex** reach its end without starting another bot.
After a special someone who had helped **Vortex** get to where it is proposed a
tribute to **Vortex** in the Reaction Roles Support server, that plan changed. The
tribute inspired the decision to begin work on a successor.

## How Axis relates to Vortex

**Axis** is intended to carry **Vortex**'s legacy forward as a new bot. It is a new
project, rather than a continuation of **Vortex**'s existing bot account. **Vortex**'s
story and history are documented separately on the [Vortex lore page](/story).

## Development status

**Axis** is in development. Its features, launch plans, and other details have not
been announced yet. This page will be updated as more information is available.

The source code for **Axis** is Open Source, it can be found [here](https://github.com/playfairs/axis).
