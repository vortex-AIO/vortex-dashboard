# Heresy, & Vortex.

This page explains the entire lore between the timeline of when **Heresy** was created, 
and to when **Vortex** was announced to be discontinued. It is not written to be professional,
but more like a story, or lore as it is.

## How did we get here?

On **September 13, 2024**, I made a Discord Bot called **Heresy**, I made this bot because
a bot I had previously used called **greed** was bought by someone, the bot went down for a while, 
this left me and the server I ran without a bot, I decided since we were without a bot, I would try
to make my own bot. So I made **Heresy**, heresy lasted a short bit of time, it had fun, **Heresy** wasn't
just a bot, it was also a community, a community built around the bot, a group of friends, a large group of friends,
an amazing group of friends, this group was the best group of friends I ever had, and nothing could ever pass that.

Then on **March 6, 2025**. **Vortex**, a new bot I decided to make, the successor of **Heresy**, a refined bot with a new
codebase, a new list of developers, a new motive, everything about **Vortex** was better than **Heresy** except one thing,
and that was simply that the community that **Heresy** was built on, was not the same community **Vortex** was built on,
**Vortex** was built with intention to surpass my previous bot, **Vortex** was a project to do better than before,
it was a successful bot, but it was also made with a completely different intention.

## So how did Vortex even pass Heresy?

Well, when I was making **Vortex** I still had a good group of friends, and I also had more motivation, it wasn't the same 
as when I was working on **Heresy**, this was different but it was also kinda egotistical, I did feel as if I could make a 
better bot that I did before, and I did, I did make a better bot, I didn't make a bot with the intention of passing bots such as 
**bleed** or **evict**, I just made the bot to pass my own initial creation. And I did that, I don't care about making the best
discord bot on the platform, the best discord bot isn't even definable, there is no such thing as a bot best at everything. 
**Vortex** didn't need that anyways, I was bad at hosting my own bot's and I still am.

So, with that in mind. **Vortex** was able to pass **Heresy** because of 2 reasons. For starters, the bot was made with more focus
and a better mindset, so it was able to be maintained a lot more, plus it had more than one person on the dev team. This was not the
main reason that **Vortex** was able to pass **Heresy**. The main reason **Vortex** was able to pass **Heresy** was because of the
**Reaction Roles Support** server. One of the best server's I had ever joined, I joined around the time guild tags were being rolled out to 
few servers, and I was pretty active in the server, after some time, **Vortex** was added to the servr per recommendation. For privacy reasons
I will not state the person who added the bot. But I am very thankful, that one person alone is the reason why **Vortex** was able to pass
**Heresy** and why I consider **Vortex** my most successful project in terms of users. The people in that server were interested in some of the
features of **Vortex** and because of that it became more used and people started adding it to their own servers, a quite popular feature of **Vortex**
is lore, a feature that was actually derived from **Heresy**, the bot was pretty quick to have lore as it's most enjoyed command, so much so
that I even updated it by a lot when I was reworking parts of the bot when Components V2 came out for Discord.py, then fastforward to October.

On **October 11, 2025**, I get banned on Discord on both of my accounts, I considered it to be a false ban, but I didn't care enough to make
a new account for a few days, on **October 17, 2025** I made a new account. But I lost interest in working on **Vortex**, because I no longer had the 
team belonging to **Vortex**. So I didn't care enough to work on the bot externally. **Vortex** continued to be used and actively running even then
and people still cared about the bot, and it's commands such as lore and for some reason afk, according to usage stats, afk is the most ran
command with **Vortex**...?'

## Where Vortex is currently heading.

As of **September 30, 2026**, **Vortex** is facing inevitable doom because of changes to the Discord Developers stuff where you have to verify bots 
every year or so and also have to apply for intents every year, me being unable to access the team, I cannot verify the bot nor can I request intents
when it loses them on **December 27, 2026**. So I have decided to continue **Vortex**'s legacy by instead creating a new bot. My initial plan was to
let the bot die, and to never make a bot again, but I was moved by the same person who helped **Vortex** get to where it is in the first place, an event
in the **Reaction Roles Support** server will take place as a tribute to **Vortex**, I was not the on who thought of it, but the person who did, had
singlehandedly changed my mind and decided to work on a new bot, I had already decided that I might do this just a few days before, but I never 
decided if I was gonna go through with it, until my mind was set that is.
